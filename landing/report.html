Report illegal content

Use this form to tell us that something on Neighbro is illegal — not that you dislike it. Anyone can send one: you do not need an account, and you do not need to be a user. Authorities can use it too.

To hide a message you would rather not see, use the complaint on the message itself. If a business refused a discount it published, complain from the offer. Those are different things and go to a different place.

A person reads every report — not an automated system. You will be told what was decided, why, and how to contest it. If the content had already disappeared before your report arrived, we will say so plainly rather than pretend to have examined it.
A chat is not stored anywhere, not even by us: it is encrypted on the two devices and passes through the server without being kept. So there is nothing on our side to look at, and a description in your own words leaves us with nothing to examine — while we are still obliged to examine it and answer you with reasons.

Paste the message itself below, from your own device, along with why you believe it is illegal. That copy is the only copy there will be.
If your report concerns the sexual abuse of children, leave the name and email empty. The law forbids us to require them, and the report is handled ahead of everything else. If life or safety is at risk, tell the police first — we are obliged to report it too, but they can act now.
I declare, in good faith, that the information in this report is accurate and complete to the best of my knowledge.
